About this policy

foxandroid provides India Finance Calculator as a free, ad-free Android application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, visit this policy page, or contact us.

The app’s offline calculations, optional sharing actions, and email support have different data flows. Each is explained below.

Information in the app

Calculator inputs are processed locally to calculate and display results. The app does not send your financial inputs, saved results, or preferences to foxandroid or a developer-operated server.

No account is required. The app does not collect advertising identifiers or usage analytics. It contains no advertising, analytics, or remote crash-reporting SDKs and does not request Android internet permission.

foxandroid does not sell your calculator information or use it for advertising.

Local storage & deleting your data

Calculator inputs and drafts, saved scenarios, favourites, and display preferences are stored locally on your device so you can return to them later. Android backup is disabled for the app.

You can remove locally stored app data at any time:

  • Use Settings → Clear data inside the app.
  • Clear the app’s storage through Android settings.
  • Uninstall the app.

These actions do not delete reports you have exported or copies you have shared. Delete those separately in the relevant destination. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete information held only on your device.

Sharing results & exporting files

If you choose Share, the app passes the selected report to Android’s sharing interface. You choose the receiving app or recipient and complete the sharing action.

If you choose Export CSV, Android’s document picker lets you choose a save location. This may be on your device or with a cloud storage provider you select.

Reports may contain the financial inputs and results shown in the report. Check the contents and destination before proceeding. Receiving apps, recipients, and storage providers handle those copies under their own policies.

Permissions & external links

The app does not ask for contacts, location, camera, microphone, or broad storage access. File exports use Android’s document picker.

Official-source links open in an external browser. Your calculator inputs are not appended to these links. The browser and destination website may process information according to their own policies.

When you email us

If you contact foxstream001@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or information you choose to send. We use these to respond and provide support. Please avoid sending sensitive financial details.

Email is handled by Gmail under Google’s Privacy Policy. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your enquiry and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

When you visit this website

This privacy-policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page does not include advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or third-party fonts.

GitHub may process technical information about your visit, such as your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from the app’s on-device calculations.

Security & children’s privacy

Keeping calculator information on your device reduces the need to transmit it. Protection of local information also depends on your device’s security and who can access it. Use a device lock and take care when exporting or sharing reports. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

The app does not require age information or personal profiles and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Revisions will appear on this page with an updated date. If an app update changes its data practices, we will update this policy before releasing that version and provide any additional notice or consent required.
