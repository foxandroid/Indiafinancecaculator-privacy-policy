Privacy Policy — India Finance Calculator

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Information Collection

India Finance Calculator processes financial calculator inputs locally on your device. The app does not collect or transmit those inputs to our servers.

Calculator inputs are used to perform calculations and display results. The app does not require an account.

The app does not share calculator inputs with us or other parties through its core functionality.

Calculator inputs are processed locally and are not stored on our servers.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not knowingly collect such information.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change.

Contact
